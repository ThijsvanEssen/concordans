# Hierarchy model

Where every bron stands, in the terms its source uses. It is the reference for the band "Behoort bij" in the readers, for the groupings (hulls) of the network and for an Overzicht. It is the same, and clickable, on the whole site.

The model is not one tree. A bron stands on one or more **axes**; on each axis it has a layer above, a same level and a layer below. Links between axes (a lid signs a stuk, a judgment cites an article) are **cross-links**, not hierarchy; Verbonden shows those.

Terms: the interface uses the term of the source (TK OData, EK, BWB, rechtspraak.nl, EUR-Lex, HUDOC), never "ouder", "kind" or "familie". Where this file says "above", "beside" and "below", that is only how the file is organised.

Status per row:

- **in data**: the relation is in the graph on production.
- **in props**: the layer is a field of the node, not a node of its own (no click target unless noted).
- **missing**: not in the data yet; the band shows nothing there and says nothing about it.

The status is that of production, read with `/api/nodes/{collection}/{key}?limit=1`, one node per type.

## Kern of context

The conclusie and the uitspraken in eerdere aanleg belong to an arrest: they are not extra data, so they do not stand in the right column. So every relation of a reader is one of two kinds:

- **Kern:** the bron cannot be read properly without it. It stands in the reading column, at the head, in legal terms, as running text.
- **Context:** it helps, but the bron reads without it. It stays in Verbonden.

One pattern for every reader: the band "Behoort bij" **is** the kern block. It does not get a second block under it, which would repeat it.

- Each line has a legal label: "Procesgang", "Conclusie", "Ingediend bij", "Bijlage bij", "Wet", "Fractie". It is followed by the bronnen as links; a chain is separated by "›".
- The groups under it (Commissies, Samenhang met, Bijlagen as a count) stay folded. A group that is kern for its reader becomes a line.
- On a phone the first line shows, and the rest folds open.

For a Kamerstuk: the bill is kern for a paper that changes or explains it (nota van wijziging, memorie van toelichting, nader rapport, nota naar aanleiding van het verslag), and it ends the dossier's line, one chain: "Dossier: 34372 computercriminaliteit III › Voorstel van wet, nr. 2". "Biedt aan" is kern for a geleidende brief or aanbiedingsbrief: the stukken it offers. The line lists them side by side ("Biedt aan: Voorstel van wet, Kamerstuk 37027, nr. 2; Memorie van toelichting, …"), and Verbonden leaves them out of "Stukken van dezelfde zaak" (`naastHetStuk` in `$lib/bron/zaak.ts`). "In dezelfde zaak" is context: a stuk reads without the other papers of its zaak, so it is in Verbonden only.

A bron's own content is neither kern nor context: a law's articles are its table of contents and its text, so they are not a group in Verbonden (`INHOUD` and `isInhoudGroep` in `src/lib/bron/behoort.ts`). The table keeps them, and within its law an article reads "Artikel 1", not the law's title again. The same holds for a fractie's leden, which its reader lists in full. Its Kamer stands once, in the band ("Kamer: Tweede Kamer"), not again in the kicker. A commissie (its officers and a count), a kabinet (a count of its bewindspersonen) and a dossier or activiteit do not list theirs in the reader, so those stay in Verbonden; the reader's list leads, Verbonden is context.

A relation in the kern does not come back as a group in Verbonden. `KERN` in `src/lib/bron/behoort.ts` lists the buckets the kern lines read, per kind of bron. `isKernGroep` tells Verbonden which of its groups read only those, and it leaves them out of its list and its count. The table keeps every connection.

- Its switch "toon in netwerk" goes with the group. A uitspraak's eerdere aanleg, conclusie and hoger beroep are on the network by default. The wet of an artikel, the dossier of a stuk and the fractie of a lid have their switch under Groepering in the legend.
- A bron in the kern is not listed again by another relation. Take the Rechtbank in the procesgang of HR Urgenda: the arrest also cites it (`REFERS_TO`), but it is not under "Uitspraken die hier worden aangehaald". This also covers the instances before the eerdere aanleg (`procesgangIds`), and a translation of a uitspraak in the kern (`translation_of`, e.g. HR 2019:2007, the English version of HR 2019:2006). A translation elsewhere says "Engelse vertaling". The table keeps every connection.
- An eerdere aanleg the data lacks (`unresolved_appeal_targets`) stands in the procesgang as text, without a link.

Per kind:

- **Lid:** the fractie line carries its period: "Fractie: Volt, sinds 2021"; an earlier fractie "VVD, 2017–2021". It is built from the lid's own `faction_memberships` (`fractiePeriodes` in `src/lib/bron/fractie-periode.ts`): records that follow on (a new role the next day) make one period, and the current fractie comes first. On a phone the period stays.
- **Motie:** "De uitslag moet duidelijk zijn, maar de kernregel gaat over de inhoud." The outcome stays out of the kern line. It is a section of its own under the head (`StemmingUitslag`), and it opens with the outcome in shape, colour and word (Uitkomst) and the seats beside it, readable without scrolling, on a phone too. Voor and Tegen carry the Stem shape.
- **Dossier:** no stage line above the dossier reader; the reader is the procedure.

## What the band shows

The band "Behoort bij" in every reader reads its three parts from this model and from nothing else:

- **Kern:** lines, each a legal term and the bronnen it names as links (a click opens in place), a chain separated by "›" (`Regel` in `behoort.ts`). An artikel: "Wet: Awb › Hoofdstuk 3 › Afdeling 3.2", each division opening at its heading. A uitspraak: "Procesgang: Rechtbank Den Haag, 24 juni 2015 › Gerechtshof Den Haag, 9 oktober 2018 › deze uitspraak"; the conclusie of the A-G or P-G stands in the status line of the reader ("Conclusie: P-G Langemeijer en Wissink, 13 september 2019"); a conclusie: "Conclusie bij: Hoge Raad, 20 december 2019". A motie or amendement: "Ingediend bij: 36496 Wet betaalbare huur". A lid: "Fractie: D66". A line that needs more than the light node gives it with `laad` and fills in when it comes (the instances before the eerdere aanleg, one light node per step, at most three).
- **Kern groups:** the band holds no context. A group stays in the band only where it is kern for its kind and too long for one line: more than one hoger beroep or cassatie against a uitspraak (the line names the first). The bijlagen of a stuk (`ACCOMPANIES inbound`) are in its status line, "Bijlagen 15", opened in place. Commissies, kabinetten, Samenhang met, Ook gepubliceerd als, In dezelfde zaak, the stukken and activiteiten of a dossier, the leden of a fractie: Verbonden.
- A layer with status **missing** is left out without a word; one **in props** shows as text, or as a link where a reader for it exists (an afdeling of a wet in the wetslezer).
- The band never holds up the first paint: its lines come from the light node and props, a line that needs more (`laad`) with its own request.
- A kern group is a group `{ id, label, aantal, bak?, laad? }` (`Groep` in `src/lib/bron/behoort.ts`). `aantal` comes from the light node (`limit=1`), null where it is not known. The rows are asked when the group is opened: the bucket `bak`, or `laad(signal)` for a list that is not one bucket, such as the stukken of a zaak. One group is open at a time; the band lists at most 50 rows, and Verbonden lists all of them.
