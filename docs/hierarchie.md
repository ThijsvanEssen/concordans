# Hierarchy model

Where every bron stands, in the terms its source uses. It is the reference for the band "Behoort bij" in the readers, for the groupings (hulls) of the network and for an Overzicht. Thijs, 9 October 2026: "maak het consistent en klikbaar zodat de hiërarchie gelijk voelt voor de hele website".

The model is not one tree. A bron stands on one or more **axes**; on each axis it has a layer above, a same level and a layer below. Links between axes (a lid signs a stuk, a judgment cites an article) are **cross-links**, not hierarchy; Verbonden shows those.

Terms: the interface uses the term of the source (TK OData, EK, BWB, rechtspraak.nl, EUR-Lex, HUDOC), never "ouder", "kind" or "familie". Where this file says "above", "beside" and "below", that is only how the file is organised.

Status per row:

- **in data**: the relation is in the graph on production.
- **in props**: the layer is a field of the node, not a node of its own (no click target unless noted).
- **missing**: not in the data yet; the band shows nothing there and says nothing about it.
- **after backfill**: the code that writes the relation is merged, production does not have it yet.

Checked against production on 9 October 2026 (API 0.79.23, `/api/nodes/{collection}/{key}?limit=1`, one node per type), and by Back-end #3 against the relation catalogue and the pipelines on develop (including #351; #353, #356 and #357 open).

## Kern of context

Thijs, 9 October 2026: the conclusie and the uitspraken in eerdere aanleg belong to an arrest; the right column reads as extra data, and this is not extra. So every relation of a reader is one of two kinds:

- **Kern:** the bron cannot be read properly without it. It stands in the reading column, at the head, in legal terms, as running text.
- **Context:** it helps, but the bron reads without it. It stays in Verbonden.

One pattern for every reader: the band "Behoort bij" **is** the kern block. It does not get a second block under it, which would repeat it.

- Each line has a legal label: "Procesgang", "Conclusie", "Ingediend bij", "Bijlage bij", "Wet", "Fractie". It is followed by the bronnen as links; a chain is separated by "›".
- The groups under it (Commissies, Samenhang met, Bijlagen as a count) stay folded. A group that is kern for its reader becomes a line.
- On a phone the first line shows, and the rest folds open.

## What the band shows

The band "Behoort bij" in every reader reads its three parts from this model and from nothing else:

- **Kern:** lines, each a legal term and the bronnen it names as links (a click opens in place), a chain separated by "›" (`Regel` in `behoort.ts`). An artikel: "Wet: Awb › Hoofdstuk 3 › Afdeling 3.2", each division opening at its heading. A uitspraak: "Procesgang: Rechtbank Den Haag, 24 juni 2015 › Gerechtshof Den Haag, 9 oktober 2018 › deze uitspraak" and "Conclusie: P-G Langemeijer en Wissink"; a conclusie: "Conclusie bij: Hoge Raad, 20 december 2019". A motie or amendement: "Ingediend bij: 36496 Wet betaalbare huur". A lid: "Fractie: D66". A line that needs more than the light node gives it with `laad` and fills in when it comes (the instances before the eerdere aanleg, one light node per step, at most three).
- **Beside:** the same level as a count per kind, one tap to the list: "In dezelfde zaak", "Bij dit debat", "Ook in deze afdeling", "Andere leden van de fractie".
  - "In dezelfde zaak" only where the stuk has a TK zaak **and** that zaak holds at least one other stuk; a Brief regering's zaak often holds only itself, and then the part is left out. Its list: at most 3 zaken, without the stuk itself, deduplicated, the stuk's substance first, other letters next, the geleidende brief or aanbiedingsbrief last (by the source's kind), then by date.
- **Below:** what falls under it where there is something: "Bijlagen", "Stemming", "Leden" (of a fractie), "Artikelen" (of a wet, as the table of contents).
  - The stemming of a motie or amendement goes through the zaak (`ABOUT` on the besluit), not through the stuk: one more request per zaak, so it loads on its own, with its own "Laden…".
  - "Bijlagen" only once #351 gives `ACCOMPANIES`; until then no part and no empty heading.
- A layer with status **missing** is left out without a word; one **in props** shows as text, or as a link where a reader for it exists (an afdeling of a wet in the wetslezer).
- The band never holds up the first paint: "Behoort bij" comes from the head (often in props), the other two parts each with their own request.
- Each part of "beside" and "below" is a group `{ id, label, aantal, bak?, laad? }` (`Groep` in `src/lib/bron/behoort.ts`). `aantal` comes from the light node (`limit=1`), null where it is not known. The rows are asked when the group is opened: the bucket `bak`, or `laad(signal)` for a list that is not one bucket, such as the stukken of a zaak. One group is open at a time; the band lists at most 50 rows, and Verbonden lists all of them.
