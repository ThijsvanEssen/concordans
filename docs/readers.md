# Readers

The reading surfaces of `/explore` for the selected bron:

| Place                    | Modes                           | Component                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reader                   | Tekst; Netwerk with a selection | `KindReader.svelte`, which picks one reader per collection                                                                                          |
| Table                    | `?tabel=1`                      | `src/lib/verbonden/VerbondenTabel.svelte`: every verbinding of the bron, sortable, fifty at a time, CSV export                                      |
| Beside the reader        | both, with `?naast=`            | `DocumentReader` with the article as `article`: a toelichting next to the article it explains                                                       |
| Table of contents (left) | Tekst, for an article or a law  | `LezenNav.svelte`: the articles of the whole law (`$lib/api/law-articles`, labelled in `$lib/bron/law-articles`), grouped by their place in the law |
| Verbonden (right)        | both                            | `src/lib/verbonden/Verbonden.svelte`: the verbindingen of the bron in groups                                                                        |

`explore/+page.svelte` places them. The page and `LezenNav` share one article list per law (`lawArticles`); the page derives `prevHref`, `nextHref` and `place` for `ArticleReader` from it. The page also loads the verbindingen of the bron once (`createVerbondenSource` in `src/lib/verbonden/data-source.ts`) and hands the same state to the panel, the table, the legend and the reader (the counts per lid, the number of wetsvoorstellen).

## Dispatch

Each reader is a chunk of its own. `explore/readers.ts` holds one `Lazy` (`$lib/util/lazy.svelte`) per collection; `KindReader.svelte` loads the one of `selection.collection` and shows it, or the failure in its place. A collection without an entry gets `FallbackReader`. The page asks the reader of the focus as soon as its collection is known (the focus, or the kind of a readable address), beside the bron's first request. The table, the filter sheet, a reader beside the article and `VersionCompare` load the same way, when first asked for.

| Collection    | Reader             | Reads                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `articles`    | `ArticleReader`    | `getArticleDetail`, `/history`, `/explained-by`; without a memorie there, `/api/dossiers/{number}/documents` |
| `judgments`   | `JudgmentReader`   | `getJudgmentDetail`                                                                                          |
| `documents`   | `DocumentReader`   | `getDocument`, with an article `/api/documents/{key}/passages`                                               |
| `dossiers`    | `DossierReader`    | `getDossier`, `getDossierTimeline`, `getChangedArticles`, `getAmendementen`                                  |
| `committees`  | `CommitteeReader`  | `getCommittee(slug)`                                                                                         |
| `members`     | `MemberReader`     | `getMember`, `getMemberVotes`, `getMemberAuthored`                                                           |
| `factions`    | `FactionReader`    | `/api/factions/{key}`                                                                                        |
| `decisions`   | `DecisionReader`   | `getDecision`, `getDecisionDocument`                                                                         |
| `cabinets`    | `CabinetReader`    | `getCabinet`; posts per ministry are in Bladeren                                                             |
| `commitments` | `CommitmentReader` | `getCommitment`                                                                                              |
| `annexes`     | `AnnexReader`      | `/api/annexes/{key}`                                                                                         |
| `activities`  | `ActivityReader`   | Nothing: the node's own props                                                                                |
| `instruments` | `InstrumentReader` | Renders `WholeLawReader`: the articles paged; with `?peildatum=` the text of that day                        |
| anything else | `FallbackReader`   | Nothing: kind, title, date, status and the source's URL from the props                                       |

The endpoints are described in the API's `api.md`. Calls go through `$lib/api/client` (see [`api-client.md`](api-client.md)).

## Structure of a reader

A reader that loads data holds its state as `view` (`loading`, `error`, `loaded`; the text readers add `empty`) and loads in an `$effect` that aborts its request, or ignores a late answer, when the selection changes. A reader depends on primitives such as the key or `bwb_id`, so a new selection object for the same bron does not reload it.

Every reader renders inside `ReaderFrame` (scroll container, empty, loading and error states, with Opnieuw after an error); Every reader uses `ReaderHead`: it builds the line above the title itself (glyph and kind as a link to its list, `SoortLink`; the words after the comma come from `kindParts`, a kind with a term in `kindTerm`), then the title with the actions (Bewaar first) floating at its right, the stand and the facts as one block under one rule, and rows; an empty row takes no room, and the head is s-5 from the text on every reader. A law that no longer holds is `Ingetrokken per …`, an article `Vervallen per …` (`statusOf(validity, 'wet')`). A reader gives data and words, never a line of its own; `place` is the article's. A band that has no lines is not drawn (`bandAlways`), and an Inhoud with fewer than two entries has no column, tab or button (`contents-state.svelte.ts`). A bron that did not load stands in the same column under its own heading (`BronFailure`). The frame is sized per mode by `explore/+page.svelte` (`.reader-frame`). `judgment-text.ts` holds the pure helpers that structure judgment text, among them `partiesByRole`: one row per role, pseudonymised parties (`[appellant 1]`) counted, the named ones listed with the alias the judgment gives them and their other roles, counsel after the parties. `_shared.ts` holds date formatting and `textProp`, which reads an untyped prop. `MemberReader` shows a person's career from `careerOf` (`src/lib/components/parliamentary/career.ts`): seats in a fractie, posts in a cabinet, and the functions they signed stukken in, each with its years.

The parliamentary readers share their requests in `src/lib/api` (`dossiers.ts`, `members.ts`, `committees.ts`). `DossierReader` shows the stand as `StandBalk` (`stand.ts`) and the procedure per Kamer (`procedure.ts`), with the Eerste Kamer's progress from `senate`; the phase and outcome labels are in `stages.ts`. Under the procedure stand the amendments as a timetable (`AmendementenDienstregeling`, `amendementen.ts`): a row per amendment as it came to the vote, a column per day on which a paper appeared (not to scale: the days cluster), the month above its days and the day under it, a rule where the month changes and a dotted one where the week does; a stop is the paper's number in a ring (its card gives kind, day, indieners and vote), and at the end the outcome as a shape with the tally, "■ 92–58". Where the column is narrower than the timetable it scrolls in its own box, the names standing (sticky). It is one request, the dossier's neighbourhood (`PART_OF`, `ABOUT`, `REVISES`, depth 2), asked once the section is in view and only where the dossier's own counts per kind hold amendments. The chain is the `REVISES` edges between papers; the vote is the decision whose `primary_case_id` is the case of the last paper. Nothing is read from a title. Without `REVISES` each paper stands alone. The count stands in the heading; no sentence sums up above the list.

Every place that names a bron by its place reads its citation record (`citationRecord`, `src/lib/text/citation-record.ts`, one function with a guard in `guards/citation-record.test.ts`): the name its heading gives it, its place as the Leidraad cites it (`art. 6:162 BW`, `Kamerstukken II 2023/24, 36306, nr. 11`, the vergaderjaar from the paper's day where the API names none, `Stb. 2018, 387`), short in a list and in full in Citeer. A publication is headed by the law it enacted, with its abbreviation (`Wet beveiliging netwerk- en informatiesystemen (Wbni), Stb. 2018, 387`, from its dossier's `enacted`), and says the day that took effect; a law by the name it goes by with its abbreviation (`Algemene verordening gegevensbescherming (AVG)`).

`ArticleReader` records the law name with `recordInstrument`, so the hover panel can show it. Its head is one grey line with the place of the article below its law (`layersOf` in `src/lib/text/division.ts`: titel and afdeling, never the law or a BW book a second time), the title as its citation record heads it (`Artikel 6:162 BW`, `Artikel 6 AVG`: the number with the abbreviation its law is cited by; a law without one by its number, its name standing above), and one line with the version picker (`/history`; `Geldend recht, tekst sinds …`, or `Vervallen per …` for an article the API marks `repealed`), the wetsvoorstellen that touch the article (a link that filters Verbonden to them) and `Citeer artikel`. The leden are a grid (`ArticleText`): the number in the margin, the text, and at the right the number of verbindingen of that lid (a link to `?lid=`) with `Citeer` and `Kopieer tekst` on hover. Where the text itself is narrower than 600px (a container query, beside the network or on a phone), that right column folds under each lid. Every onderdeel, at any depth, shows the same two actions in that column, level with its own text. `Citeer` offers the three forms of the article's citation record (`citationRecord` in `src/lib/text/citation-record.ts`, built by `citeArticle` in `src/lib/text/cite.ts`): kort (`art. 5 lid 1 onder d sub i Sr`), voluit (`artikel 5, eerste lid, onderdeel d, onder i, Wetboek van Strafrecht`, after the Aanwijzingen voor de regelgeving) and met datum; the short and long forms are built from the abbreviation the instrument is cited by (`abbreviation`, else its short title) and its citation title, a book of the BW cited as `art. 7:3 BW` (never the API's short title of the book, `BW7`). `Kopieer tekst` copies the part's text with its onderdelen. The whole-law reader offers the same actions. A wetsvoorstel row of the panel can show its proposed wording next to the current lid (`proposed-text.ts`, from the mutations of its dossier).

**Collapsible sections.** Every section heading of a reader (`Sectie`, rung 2) is itself the button that opens and closes the section (`FoldButton`): `aria-expanded`, a quiet chevron (`Caret`), Enter and Space. What the reader chose is remembered per kind of heading, not per bron, in `localStorage` under `concordans:kopjes` (`src/lib/components/ui/dicht.svelte.ts`, a map from key to open, read and written in try/catch); the key is the heading text or the `sleutel` prop, and a section that something else opens has its key in `FOLD` (Amendementen, opened from a day of votes in the procedure, by the key that is also its anchor). The legend and the panel Netwerk over the canvas are kept there too. A section starts closed; its count stands in the heading. A closed section renders nothing, so it loads nothing; the amendments ask their neighbourhood once in view, so their count is there while closed. Sub-headings fold too and start open: the groups of a dossier's procedure (`Tijdlijn inklapbaar`, key `sub:<titel>`) and the laws of Gewijzigde artikelen (`sub:wet:<id>`). Verbonden's groups start open, key `verbonden:<group id>`. A list that is the page itself (the index overview) passes `inklapbaar={false}`.

**A row of Verbonden** says its verbinding at a glance, from `hoeVan` in `src/lib/verbonden/hoe.ts`: one line with the verb fixed per relation and direction (its words in `relation-words.ts`: `Haalt aan`, `Aangehaald`, `Verwijst naar`, `Wijzigt`, `Door de Kamer verbonden`, a vote's outcome) and the rest from the edge (`Row.edge`, its meta): the lid, the overwegingen (`in r.o. 4.3 en 5.1`), the count, the words of a law, the day in force, the tally. Below it the passage with the words that name the selected bron marked, where the edge has one. A verbinding below high confidence (`confidenceWord`) is in italics with "naam herkend". Where it comes from (`Staat letterlijk in de tekst`, `Wetten.overheid.nl, Stb. 2024, 193`, `Rechtspraak.nl, formele relatie`) floats under the row on hover and focus, and after a tap on a phone; it never takes a line of its own. The meta line keeps what the line does not say (`metaParts(row, true)`). The row has no panel: a click anywhere on it opens the bron in Tekst; `+` puts it among the chosen bronnen. The overwegingen of a judgment that cites an article come from its detail, which the loader asks anyway; the edge's `paragraphs` take over where the API gives them (judgment to judgment waits for that). The table keeps its rows that open under themselves.

**Verbonden at a glance.** In Tekst Verbonden opens on its soorten (`summary.ts`, `KindSummary.svelte`; mockups V1): Uitspraken, Kamerstukken, Wetten, Dossiers, Besluiten, Activiteiten, Toezeggingen, Kamerzaken and the bodies, in the order of the mockup of the bron's kind (a dossier Wetten first, a Kamerlid Kamerstukken first). A soort is the groups whose rows are of its collection (`kindOfGroup`); its count is the larger of what its groups count and what the API counts in its buckets (`VerbondenState.buckets`), the buckets of the kern and of groups the reader shows left out. The heading of Verbonden is their sum, without "meer dan". Each soort shows its heading with the count, for the judgments of an article the bar per instantie, three rows as a title and one line (`shortLine`: `r.o. 3.4, 168 keer aangehaald`, a Kamerstuk's kind and day, an article's law) and, where the soort holds more than the three rows, `Alle N …` (with nothing more to open there is no link; the count in the heading is the number alone, without a unit). A click on a title opens the row in place with its passage, its identifier and its source; the bron is one step further. `Alle N` opens the soort with its groups, its order, its filter and Per soort, `Alle soorten` goes back; a bronfilter in the address shows the groups at once. Beside the canvas Verbonden keeps its groups.

**Netwerk tonen.** In Tekst the head of Verbonden holds Netwerk tonen (`?toon=netwerk`, a step Back undoes; Netwerk verbergen takes it out). It draws the soorten above them (`NetworkSketch.svelte`, laid out by `sketch.ts`): the bron in the middle, the bronnen around it. Up to 40 (`SKETCH_LIMIT`) each stands on its own; beyond that the largest soorten become one circle with their count, the judgments that cite an article one per instantie (`tiers.ts`: Hoge Raad, Gerechtshoven, Rechtbanken from `/cited-by?tier=`, the rest Overige instanties). A soort whose rows are not all there stands as one circle. A name is never cut: it wraps at its words, 24 characters a line (14 under a circle), stays inside the drawing, and is left to its title where it would cover another name or a circle. Under it one line counts what is drawn: `13 afzonderlijk, 5 groepen, 10.517 in totaal` (besluit 29.3); the bronnen on their own and the groups' total together are the heading's count. A bron on its own opens in place on a click, its row of Verbonden under the drawing (the bron one step further); a circle opens its soort; In het netwerk under it opens the werkplek (`?mode=netwerk`).

**The order of a group.** The three rows a group shows are the three to read. The judgments that cite an article start with the most cited (`citation_count`, the API's order over all of them, `startOptions` in `loaders.ts`); a law's start with the newest, as the API's other order of a law times out on the Awb. Where the API ranks nothing, `importance.ts` orders the rows it has, by one of five rules, each from fields the rows already carry: `cited` (`inbound_citation_count`, then the newest), `papers` (a Kamerstuk by its place in the making of a law: voorstel van wet, memorie van toelichting, nota van wijziging, nota naar aanleiding van het verslag, verslag, amendement, motie, brief, the rest, then the newest), `current` (a seat still held, then the one that ended last), `office` (a bewindspersoon by the highest post: minister-president, viceminister-president, minister, minister zonder portefeuille, staatssecretaris, then the one still serving) and `newest`. A group whose order says something (changes, a vote by its choice) keeps it.

**Every kind the same.** Besluit 32 holds for every kind of bron with a page: it opens in Tekst at its bare address, Verbonden is the summary per soort (the count, the groups with their three), the network is Netwerk tonen, and on a phone the text and Verbonden stand under each other. `e2e/reizen/v1-alle-soorten.spec.ts` walks all of them (wet, artikel, bijlage, verdrag, EU-akte, publicatie, uitspraak, dossier, Kamerstuk, motie, zaak, activiteit, stemming, toezegging, Kamerlid, bewindspersoon, fractie, commissie, kabinet) at 1440 and 375; `guards/v1-overal.test.ts` requires of every kind groups in Verbonden, a link that names no network, and for every group a rule of `importance.ts` or a place in the guard's list of groups the API orders.

| Kind of bron                                 | The three of a group                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wet                                          | Dossiers: the API's; delegated regulations and related laws: most cited; changes (Staatsblad): newest; implementing acts: most cited     |
| Artikel                                      | Uitspraken: most cited (API); articles that cite or base on it: most cited; Kamerstukken: by place in the making of a law                |
| Bijlage                                      | Articles whose scope the annex gives (API order); the law is the kern line                                                               |
| Uitspraak                                    | Articles it cites and judgments it cites or that cite it: most cited                                                                     |
| Dossier                                      | Stukken per Kamer: by place in the making of a law; commitments and Eerste Kamer votes: newest; the rest the API's                       |
| Kamerstuk, motie                             | Names and named by: by place in the making of a law; explains and refers to: most cited                                                  |
| Zaak                                         | Stukken: by place in the making of a law; votes: newest                                                                                  |
| Kamerlid, bewindspersoon                     | Stukken signed per capacity: newest; dossiers and laws the lid worked on: the API's                                                      |
| Fractie                                      | Stukken it submitted: newest; dossiers and laws: the API's                                                                               |
| Commissie                                    | Leden: still seated first, then the last to leave; dossiers and activities: the API's                                                    |
| Kabinet                                      | Bewindspersonen: by the highest post, then the one still serving                                                                         |
| Activiteit, stemming, toezegging, publicatie | The API's order (a debate's records, motions and commitments; a vote's fracties by choice and seats; the articles a publication changes) |

Where the API has no rank, the front end does not invent one: the first rows are the API's, and the rank is a follow-up of the API (see `code-eenvoud/onderzoek/A159-inventaris.md`).

## Text and anchors

**Parts.** `ArticleText` (`src/lib/components/articles`) renders an article as aanhef, leden and onderdelen, each with an anchor id (`aanhef`, `lid-2`, `lid-2-onder-a`) and a permalink. The parts come from the API, or are read from the text where it gives none (`partsOf` in `src/lib/text/article-parts.ts`); the card of an article shows the same parts.

**Citations.** `CitedText` turns the citation spans of an article or judgment into links, at the lid or onderdeel the source names: the qualifier comes from the span (`qualifierOfSpan`) or from the text around it (`parseQualifier` in `src/lib/text/qualifier.ts`). A link to the article on screen is a jump within the page. Hovering shows the cited part.

**Defined terms.** Where an article uses a term its regulation defines (`term_spans` and `definitions` on `/api/articles/{bwb_id}/{article_number}`), `CitedText` makes the term a reference too, with a quiet dotted underline (`a.begrip`). Its card is the hover card of every reference (BronLink `voorbeeld`): `Begrip, art. 1, onder b`, the term, the definition as the regulation writes it; a click opens the article that gives it. A citation whose words are such a term (`definition_ref`: "artikel 11 van de wet") carries the definition as one line more on its card (BronLink `extra`). The terms come with the article, so no request more. Helpers in `src/lib/text/begrippen.ts`.

**Overwegingen.** `JudgmentReader` gives each numbered paragraph the id `paragraph_id` from the API or `rov-{number}`, with a permalink. An unnumbered paragraph keeps its `paragraph_id` (positional, `p-21`), so a citation of it lands.

**Passages.** `DocumentReader` opened beside an article lands on the passage for it: from `/api/documents/{key}/passages`, or a heading found in the text (`findPassage` in `src/lib/text/find-passage.ts`, marked as derived).

**Landing.** `src/lib/bron/landing.ts`: `anchorOfHash` reads the fragment, `landOn` scrolls the element into view and marks it until the next click, `permalink` builds the address of a position.

## Navigation

Links inside a reader are `bronHref` (see [`routes.md`](routes.md#links-to-a-bron)); a reader has no navigation callback. A reader never stands beside the canvas: the network is a werkplek of its own (`?mode=netwerk`, besluit 39), and in Tekst the network is Netwerk tonen in Verbonden. A new reader needs no change for either. There is no bar under the TopBar.

## Adding a reader

1. Create `explore/readers/{Kind}Reader.svelte` with `node: GraphNode` (or `selection`) as prop and a `view` state.
2. Add it to `READERS` in `explore/readers.ts`, and its address kind to `COLLECTION_OF_PAD`.
3. For a text source, use `ReaderFrame` and `ReaderHead`, and give the parts a reference can point at an id.
4. Add the groups of its verbindingen to `GROUPS` in `src/lib/verbonden/groups.ts`.
5. The kind itself is added as described in [`graph-system.md`](graph-system.md#adding-a-node-kind).
